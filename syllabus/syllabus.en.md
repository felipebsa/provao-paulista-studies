# Provão Paulista syllabus

[Português](syllabus.pt-BR.md)

Full list of what can be on the Provão Paulista Seriado, split by subject and by high school year, based on the official syllabus. ✅ means I have already studied the topic; topics without a mark I haven't studied yet.

In the 1st, 2nd and 3rd columns, ● shows which year the topic is on. Sociology only starts in the 2nd year, and philosophy is not on the 2nd year exam.
