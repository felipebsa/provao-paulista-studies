# Provão Paulista Studies

[Português](README.pt-BR.md)

My study notes for the Provão Paulista Seriado, the serial entrance exam for public high school students in São Paulo state (USP, Unicamp, Unesp, Fatec and Univesp).

I write down what I have already studied, in my own words, and keep track of the mistakes I make in past exams. The full syllabus is here too, with a mark on every topic I've studied.

## How it's organized

- `notes/`: one numbered folder per topic, in the order I studied it. Every note exists in two languages: `.en.md` for English and `.pt-BR.md` for Portuguese.
- `syllabus/`: everything that can be on the exam, by subject and year, with ✅ on what I've studied.
- `exams/`: official past exams and answer keys, organized by year and grade.
- `practice/`: practice tests in the exam's style.
- `notes/learnings.*.md`: mistakes I made and how to fix them, newest on top.

## How I study

I take a past exam with a timer, grade it, and split my mistakes into three kinds: I didn't know the content, I didn't understand the question, or it was a careless slip. Then I study the content I was missing, write the note here, and log the mistake in `learnings`. I prefer reading over videos, so most of what's here comes from the exams themselves and written explanations.
