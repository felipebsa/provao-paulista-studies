## The marks I use

While I answer, I mark the doubts next to the letter:

- `?` a lot of doubt, almost a guess.
- `!` I'm not sure.
- `-` I didn't understand a word in the statement or in an option.

| Mark | Questions | Right |
|---|---|---|
| `?` | 1 | 1 |
| `!` | 17 | 14 |
| `-` | 20 | 13 |
| any mark | 36 | 27 |
| no mark | 54 | 52 |

9 of the 11 I missed had a mark, and 7 of them had a word I didn't understand.
