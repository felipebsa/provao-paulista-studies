# Biology (1st year)

## Energy metabolism

| Process | Needs O₂? | Goes in | Comes out | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Photosynthesis | no (makes O₂) | CO₂ + water + light | glucose + O₂ | stored |
| Aerobic respiration | yes | glucose + O₂ | CO₂ + water | a lot (ATP) |
| Alcoholic fermentation | no | glucose | alcohol + CO₂ | little |
| Lactic fermentation | no | glucose | lactic acid | little |
| Chemosynthesis | depends | inorganic substances | organic matter | no light |

- Yeast does alcoholic fermentation. The CO₂ is what makes bread rise.
- Lactobacilli and muscles without oxygen do lactic fermentation.
- Chlorophyll sits in the thylakoids and absorbs light.

## Eutrophication

It goes in order, like dominoes:

1. Rain carries fertilizer (nitrogen and phosphorus) into a lake. This is called leaching.
2. Algae grow too much.
3. They cover the surface and block the light.
4. The algae die.
5. Decomposing bacteria use up a lot of oxygen breaking everything down.
6. Fish die from lack of O₂.

Fish die from lack of O₂, never of CO₂. Sewage rich in organic matter causes the same thing, just without the algae step.

## Ecology

- Producer (does photosynthesis) → primary consumer → secondary → tertiary.
- Energy goes down at each trophic level.
- Bioaccumulation: a substance that stays in the body gets more concentrated at the top of the food chain.
- Nitrogen cycle: nitrogen-fixing bacteria (in the roots of legumes, like beans) turn N₂ from the air into compounds plants can use.
- Ecological succession: it starts with pioneer species and the environment gets more complex over time.

## Cells and transport

- Prokaryote (bacteria): no organized nucleus, the DNA is in the cytoplasm.
- Eukaryote: has a nucleus and organelles.
- Osmosis: water moves from the less concentrated side to the more concentrated side, without spending energy. That's why salt preserves dried meat: the medium becomes hypertonic and microorganisms lose water.

## Classification

- There are three domains: Bacteria, Archaea and Eukarya.
- Plantae, Animalia, Fungi, Protista and Monera are kingdoms.
- A fungus (mushroom) is heterotrophic and belongs to Eukarya.
- Monophyletic group (clade): one ancestor and all of its descendants.

## Genetics

- Aa × Aa: 1/4 AA, 2/4 Aa, 1/4 aa.
- Blood type O is the universal donor, AB is the universal recipient.
- Pedigree: an unaffected child of an aa parent is definitely Aa.
- Probability with several conditions: multiply everything. Example from 2024: father definitely Aa (1) × mother with a 1/25 chance of being Aa × 1/4 chance of an aa child = 1/100.
- For "a girl and affected", also multiply by 1/2.

## Evolution

- Lamarck: use and disuse + inheritance of acquired traits (the giraffe stretching its neck).
- Darwin and Wallace: natural selection. The environment selects variations that already existed.
- Modern synthesis: Darwin + Mendel's genetics. Mutation is random, the environment only selects.
- Identical fossils on different continents show they were once joined.

## Scientific method

Observation → hypothesis ("it could be that", "maybe") → experiment → result → conclusion.

## Physiology that has been on the exam

- Mammal breathing: alveoli, diaphragm and intercostal muscles.
- Insects have an open circulatory system.
- Hormones like GLP-1 involve the endocrine, nervous and digestive systems at the same time.
