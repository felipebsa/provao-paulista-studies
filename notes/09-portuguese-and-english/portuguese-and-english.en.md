# Portuguese and English (1st year)

I already do well on reading comprehension. What costs points is grammar and narrator analysis.

## Connectives

Every year the exam asks to replace a connective "without changing the meaning".

| Meaning | Connectives |
|---|---|
| Contrast | mas, porém, contudo, todavia, no entanto, só que |
| Cause | pois, porque, já que, visto que, como (at the start of the sentence) |
| Conclusion | portanto, logo, então, por isso |
| Concession | embora, ainda que, mesmo que, apesar de |
| Condition | se, caso, desde que |
| Purpose | para que, a fim de (que) |

## Prefixes

- pre- and ante- = before (predestinada)
- des- and in- = negation (desconfiar, incerto)
- re- = repetition (refazer)

## Verb moods

- Indicative: certainty (ele esteve, ela esperou).
- Subjunctive: doubt or hypothesis. It comes after "que", "se" and "embora" (que nos saiamos, embora desempenhe).
- Imperative: command (pegue, faça).

## Agreement with impersonal verbs

"Fazer" for time and "haver" meaning "to exist" have no subject, so they stay singular: "faz dois anos", "houve problemas". "Existir" does agree: "existem pessoas".

## Narrator

- Talks to the reader when "vós", "você" or "leitor" shows up.
- Narrator's and character's voices in the same passage: the line with a dash + "disse Adélia".
- Omniscience failing: the narrator says "I don't know" or "maybe".
- Metalanguage: the narrator comments on the story itself ("the hero of this story").

## Figures of speech

- Simile has "como". Metaphor doesn't.
- Hyperbole: exaggeration.
- Personification: a human action given to something that isn't human ("the wind was singing").
- Synesthesia: mixing senses ("singing flowers, fragrant birds").
- The exam already puts the definition in the option, so reading carefully is enough without memorizing.

## Weak rhyme (rima pobre)

A rhyme between words of the same word class (two nouns, like "agonias" and "dias"). I classify each word before marking.

## Literature

| Movement | Main trait |
|---|---|
| Troubadour poetry | cantiga de amigo (female voice, missing the beloved, refrain) and cantiga de amor (male voice, unreachable lady) |
| Baroque | conflict between faith and pleasure, antithesis and paradox (Gregório de Matos) |
| Arcadianism | countryside, shepherds, Greek mythology, simple language |
| Romanticism | intense emotion, the Indigenous hero |
| Realism | objective social criticism |
| Modernism | free verse, everyday language |

Arcadian mottos: fugere urbem (flee the city), locus amoenus (pleasant place), carpe diem (seize the day), aurea mediocritas (simple, balanced life), inutilia truncat (cut what is useless).

## English

Only reading comprehension. The wrong option sounds logical but goes beyond the text (it swaps "more than half" for "all", or "around the country" for "around the world"). Markers worth knowing:

- However = no entanto
- Still / Yet = ainda assim
- Although / Though = embora
- Therefore = portanto
- Moreover = além disso
