- **Subject:** Mathematics
- **What I got wrong / learned:** I didn't know how to find the roots of f(x + 2) from f(x).
- **How to fix it:** replace every x with (x + 2) inside the function. f(x + k) moves left and f(x − k) moves right.

- **Date:** 2026-11-01
