# How the Provão Paulista works

Provão Paulista Seriado is the serial entrance exam for public high school students in São Paulo state. It gives access to USP, Unicamp, Unesp, Fatec and Univesp, and it is run by Fundação Vunesp.

## The three exams

Each high school year takes one exam at the end of the year, over two days:

| Exam | Year | What it has |
|---|---|---|
| Provão Paulista I | 1st year | 90 multiple choice questions |
| Provão Paulista II | 2nd year | 90 multiple choice questions |
| Provão Paulista III | 3rd year | 90 multiple choice questions + essay |

If you miss one of the stages, you are disqualified and can't take the next year's exam.

## The two booklets

In Provão I:

| Day | Booklet | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | Languages + Natural Sciences | 18 Portuguese, 6 English, 8 physics, 8 chemistry, 8 biology (48) |
| 2nd | Mathematics + Human Sciences | 18 math, 8 history, 8 geography, 8 philosophy (42) |

There is no sociology in the 1st year. In the 2nd year philosophy leaves and sociology comes in, and the 3rd year has both.

Every question has 5 options. In Provão I the exam runs from 8 a.m. to noon, so you get 4 hours per booklet, about 5 minutes per question.

## How the final score is built

For students who started high school in 2026 (2026 edital):

| Stage | Weight |
|---|---|
| Provão I (multiple choice) | 25% |
| Provão II (multiple choice) | 25% |
| Provão III (multiple choice) | 30% |
| Provão III (essay) | 20% |

The 1st year counts, but the 3rd year plus the essay is half the final score.
