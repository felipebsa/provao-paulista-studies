# History and geography (1st year)

In both, the answer is almost always a paraphrase of the text in the question. Still, some topics come up again and again.

## History

- Athenian democracy: direct, but only adult men whose father and mother were Athenian were citizens. Women, foreigners and enslaved people were left out.
- Feudalism: suzerainty and vassalage between nobles. Serfs were tied to the land and owed corvée (working the lord's land), tallage (part of the harvest) and banalities (fees for the mill and the oven). A serf was not a slave and couldn't be sold.
- Crop rotation in the Middle Ages: keep the soil fertile and grow more food.
- Renaissance: anthropocentrism, reason, observation, perspective in painting. Religious themes stayed.
- Mercantilism: bullionism (piling up gold and silver), a favorable balance of trade, protectionism, State intervention and the colonial pact. The colony could only trade with the mother country and existed to make it money.
- Liberalism (Adam Smith) is the opposite: free market and the State staying out.
- Enlightenment: Montesquieu and the separation of the three powers. The contradiction between talking about freedom and keeping slavery (US independence, Inconfidência Mineira).
- Industrial Revolution: rural exodus, workers breaking machines (Luddism), a new rhythm of time that no longer followed nature.

In 2024 there were also topics that belong to the 2nd and 3rd years:

- 19th century imperialism: colonies for raw materials and as consumer markets.
- The UN grew after World War II because of the decolonization of Africa and Asia.
- 1988 Constitution: it recognized Indigenous peoples' rights to the lands they traditionally occupy.

## Geography

- Time zones: 15° = 1 hour. East is ahead. Brasília is Greenwich − 3h.
- Parallels define latitude (0° to 90°, north and south) and climate zones. Meridians define longitude (0° to 180°, east and west) and time zones.
- A graphic scale is a drawn ruler. A numeric scale is like 1 : 100,000.
- Thematic maps: population density shown by colored areas is zonal and ordered; highways are linear.
- Cartogram (anamorfose): a map that distorts country sizes to show some data (population, GDP).
- A watershed divide is the high ground that separates two basins.
- Climate factors in Brazil, in order of importance: latitude, altitude and maritime/continental influence. Two cities at the same latitude with different climates: it's the altitude.
- Physical weathering breaks rock without changing its composition (temperature changes). Chemical weathering changes the minerals.
- Crop rotation, no-till farming and agroforestry protect the soil.
- Urban heat islands: planting trees reduces them.
