# What repeats in past exams (1st year)

I read the Provão I exams from 2023, 2024 and 2025 to see what shows up every year. When I write "3/3", it means it was on all three exams.

## Mathematics

- Scientific notation (3/3). There is always an option with the right value in the wrong format (like 653 · 10⁶).
- Vertex of a parabola (3/3): maximum height, minimum cost, maximum profit.
- Percentages and unit conversion (3/3): hectares to m², g to kg, discount coupons with a condition.
- Plane geometry (3/3): areas, similarity, circumference, angles of regular polygons, tilings.
- Reading graphs and tables, on every exam.
- Time zones show up in geography, but they are a calculation (2023 and 2025).

## Chemistry

- Concentration (3/3), mostly how much substance is in a dose.
- Atomic models (3/3). When the question talks about light and color (fireworks, flares), the answer is Rutherford-Bohr and electrons.
- Safety pictograms: flammable or corrosive.
- Separating mixtures based on a table of properties.
- Ions and ionic formulas, like Mg²⁺ with Cl⁻ forming MgCl₂.
- Stoichiometry with an excess reagent (2025).

## Biology

- Nitrogen cycle and eutrophication (2023, 2024, 2025).
- Metabolism: photosynthesis, respiration and fermentation.
- Evolution theories with the authors swapped in the options.
- Phylogenetic trees and monophyletic groups.
- Scientific method: telling apart observation, hypothesis, experiment and conclusion.
- Pedigree with probability (2024).

## Physics

It's almost never the bare formula. It always comes inside a story:

- Mechanical energy (mgh): aqueduct, tower crane, hydroelectric plant.
- Newton's laws, mostly action and reaction.
- Uniform and uniformly accelerated motion, with velocity graphs where the displacement can be zero.
- Gravitation: the force gets bigger when the distance gets smaller.
- Power: energy ÷ time.

## Portuguese

- Comic strips with a figure of speech (3/3). The definition of the figure is written in the option itself.
- Replacing a connective without changing the meaning (3/3).
- What a pronoun refers to (3/3).
- Figurative meaning ("estar com a bola toda").
- Sonnets: Arcadian in 2023 and 2024, Baroque in 2025, plus a cantiga de amigo in 2025.
- Narrator: when they talk to the reader, when both voices appear.

## English

Only reading comprehension. The wrong option usually looks right, but the text doesn't say that.

## History

- Athenian democracy and its limits (2024 and 2025, with almost the same answer).
- Mercantilism and the colonial pact.
- The Enlightenment and Montesquieu.
- Industrial Revolution: machine breaking, rural exodus.
- It's almost always an excerpt from a historian followed by "the text shows that".

## Geography

- Time zones.
- Cartography: graphic and numeric scale, parallels and meridians, how a thematic map shows data.
- Morphoclimatic domains.
- River basins.
- Climate factors.

## Philosophy

- Common sense and science (3/3).
- Hobbes (2023 and 2025), with Locke and Rousseau as distractors.
- Descartes and Socrates.
- Ethics: bioethics, alterity, the imperative of responsibility.
- There is almost always an excerpt from Marilena Chauí or Maria Lúcia de Arruda Aranha, and the answer is in the text itself.
