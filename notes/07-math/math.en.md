# Mathematics (1st year)

## Numbers and percentages

| What | Formula or rule |
|---|---|
| Percentage | x% of V = x ÷ 100 × V |
| Increase of i% | V × (1 + i). E.g. +10% → × 1.10 |
| Discount of i% | V × (1 − i). E.g. −25% → × 0.75 |
| Successive increases and discounts | multiply one after the other. +10% and then −10% doesn't go back to the original price |
| Percentage of a percentage | multiply: 30% of 45% = 0.30 × 0.45 = 13.5% |
| Percentage change | (new − old) ÷ old |
| Interest on financing | interest ÷ amount financed (what was still owed, not the full price) |
| Scientific notation | a × 10ⁿ, with a between 1 and 10 |
| Scale | drawing ÷ real (1 : 100,000 → 1 cm = 1 km) |
| Inverse proportionality | x × y always gives the same number |
| Arithmetic progression | aₙ = a₁ + (n − 1) · r |

## Functions

| What | Formula |
|---|---|
| Linear function | f(x) = ax + b. a is how much it grows per unit and b is the fixed value |
| Slope from a line | a = Δy ÷ Δx |
| Root of a linear function | x = −b ÷ a |
| Discriminant | Δ = b² − 4ac |
| Quadratic formula | x = (−b ± √Δ) ÷ 2a |
| Sum and product of roots | sum = −b ÷ a, product = c ÷ a |
| x of the vertex | xv = −b ÷ 2a (where the peak is) |
| y of the vertex | yv = f(xv) or −Δ ÷ 4a (how high the peak is) |
| Maximum or minimum | a < 0 has a maximum, a > 0 has a minimum |

### Parabola: what each number means

With a kicked ball h(x) = −x² + 8x:

- xv = 4 is where, along the ground, the ball is highest.
- h(4) = 16 is the maximum height.
- The roots (0 and 8) are where it touches the ground.

A parabola is symmetric, so the vertex sits right between the roots. "Maximum height", "minimum cost" and "maximum profit" ask for the value (yv). "How many students", "at what moment" and "how far" ask for xv.

### Shifting a graph

f(x + k) moves k units to the left and f(x − k) moves to the right. To find the new roots, I replace every x with (x + k) inside the function.

### Comparing two functions

To find when one car rental becomes cheaper than another, I set them equal (120 + 0.8x = 80 + 1.2x → x = 100) and test a bigger value.

## Geometry

| What | Formula |
|---|---|
| Square / rectangle | s² / base × height |
| Triangle | base × height ÷ 2 |
| Trapezoid | (B + b) × h ÷ 2 |
| Rhombus | D × d ÷ 2 |
| Circle | area = πr², circumference = 2πr |
| Pythagoras | a² = b² + c² (a is the hypotenuse) |
| Sum of interior angles | (n − 2) × 180° |
| Angle of a regular polygon | (n − 2) × 180° ÷ n |
| Volume of a rectangular box | length × width × height |
| Similarity | sides and perimeter × k, area × k² |
| Midpoint | ((x₁ + x₂) ÷ 2 , (y₁ + y₂) ÷ 2) |
| Trigonometry | sin = opposite ÷ hypotenuse, cos = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse, tan = opposite ÷ adjacent |

### Similarity (shadows)

I build a table in columns, height with height and shadow with shadow. A 1.8 m person with a 2 m shadow, a pole with a 6 m shadow: 1.8/x = 2/6, so x = 5.4 m. I never cross height with shadow.

### Tilings

Around a point, the angles of the tiles add up to 360°. Two octagons (135° each) make 270°, so a square (90°) is missing. On their own, only the triangle, the square and the hexagon tile a plane, because their angle divides 360 exactly.

### Hidden perimeter

When the question talks about going around, surrounding, fencing or wrapping the edge, it's perimeter, even without the word "perimeter".

### Tank with a divider

I split the calculation into before and after the water spills over. Example: a 1,500 cm² base and a flow of 150 cm³/s gives 1,500 ÷ 150 = 10 s per 1 cm. I check the units to see if the calculation makes sense (cm³ ÷ cm³/s gives seconds).

## Statistics and units

- Mean: sum ÷ count. Median: the middle value with the data in order (if there's an even count, the mean of the two middle ones). Mode: the most frequent value.
- Pie charts: 1% = 3.6°.
- 1 ha = 10,000 m², 1 m³ = 1,000 L, 1 L = 1,000 mL = 1 dm³, 1 kg = 1,000 g.
- Population density = inhabitants ÷ area.
- Decimal hours × 60 = minutes (1.25 h = 1h15, not 1h25).
